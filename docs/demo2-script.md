# Ratib Demo 2 script

## 00:00-00:12 - Opening

We did not begin with a feature list. We asked how businesses actually operate digital assets. The research found a repeatable finance workflow. Privy made it buildable as Ratib.

## 00:12-00:30 - Research journey

Our first report mapped 27 products. The second compared Ratib with 20 useful benchmarks. The third traced those lessons into the working product. Each iteration moved us from market evidence toward an executable finance workflow.

## 00:30-00:48 - The landscape

The market was deep but divided. Finance products handled operations, wallet platforms controlled authority, accounting products proved what happened, and infrastructure platforms connected systems. No category owned the complete business loop.

## 00:48-01:05 - The recurring pattern

Across those different products, the same pattern kept appearing: prepare, review, authorize, settle, reconcile, and report. The category leaders were solving different moments in one shared operating loop.

## 01:05-01:20 - The question

That raised the real design question: could one product connect the complete business workflow without giving itself uncontrolled wallet authority?

## 01:20-01:42 - The answer: Privy

Privy made that possible. Identity establishes the human actor. Embedded wallets hold and sign. Quorums, policies, authorization signatures, MFA, intents, and events independently enforce what the product is allowed to do.

## 01:42-02:00 - Authority architecture

Ratib authenticates the operator, scopes the organization, and prepares the workflow. Privy decides whether the wallet action is authorized. Base Sepolia settles it. Convex records the evidence. Ratib roles never become signing rights by accident.

## 02:00-02:24 - Proven patterns adopted

With that authority foundation, we adopted workflow patterns, never proprietary code. Finance products shaped payroll and payouts. Wallet platforms shaped approvals and policy. Accounting platforms shaped reconciliation and close. Infrastructure platforms shaped reliable developer operations.

## 02:24-02:53 - Ratib today

Those patterns now meet inside Ratib. Finance prepares the operation, Privy authorizes it, Base Sepolia settles it, accounting preserves the evidence, and ERPNext and the developer API connect it to the business.

## 02:53-03:00 - Close

Privy is not the business workflow. It is what made the workflow possible. Ratib is the proof that wallet authority can become a coherent B2B finance operating system.
